WHY THIS WAS STILL WORTH RUNNING
Model149 showed a weaker retrained member can help through decorrelation, so the
pattern was not obvious in advance. It fails here because Model153 and Model140
are the same architecture AND the same recipe, differing only in label round -
the same near-duplicate problem as Model148 and Model151. The Kraken variants got
away with it because that member is far enough from the rest of the ensemble that
even its own variants disagree usefully.

Round-5 pseudo-labels have now been tried on four architectures:
  Qwen3-VL-4B (Model145)  +0.0046 standalone, helped the ensemble
  Qwen3-VL-8B (Model147)  +0.0055 standalone, helped slightly
  GOT-OCR2    (Model148)  +0.0001 standalone, HURT the ensemble
  LightOnOCR  (Model153)  -0.0004 standalone, no ensemble gain
Whether pseudo-labels pay is a property of the architecture, not of the labels.

HOST TRAP worth recording: setup_host.sh skips image preparation when

so training started and died on the first missing file. Check the archive size,
not just the directory's presence.
