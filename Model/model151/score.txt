Retraining one architecture on different data buys decorrelation, but only until
the variants start agreeing with each other more than they disagree with the rest
of the ensemble. Three is the useful number here; the fourth is Model148 again -
a near-duplicate whose correlated vote outweighs what it adds.

Together with Model148 (a GOT-OCR2 twin, -0.00075) and Model147 (the strongest
single member, +0.0004 only), the full picture: ensemble gains come from
decorrelation, decorrelation saturates, and neither member quality nor member
count predicts the gain on its own.
