Subject: Clarification on pretrained handwriting-generator augmentation

May we use an openly available pretrained handwriting image generator, run
privately with open-source code, to generate additional training images from
the supplied Train.csv transcriptions and supplied training-image style crops?

The proposed model is DiffusionPen. We would download only its publicly
available model weights and required dependencies, not IAM or any other external
dataset, reference image, font dataset, text corpus or pre-generated images.
All generation, filtering and training would be automatic and reproducible.
We would preserve the supplied spelling and punctuation and use no manual
annotations, manually selected fixes or test images in generation.

The StackMix ruling explicitly approves crop recombination. Does permission
also extend to this pretrained generative approach, or is it prohibited?
