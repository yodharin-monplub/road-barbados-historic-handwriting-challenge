Two things learned:
 - The pseudo-label ladder is architecture-specific. It paid +0.0046 on Qwen3-VL-4B
   and +0.0055 on Qwen3-VL-8B, but nothing on GOT-OCR2, whose weighted CER barely
   moves with better targets. Predicting which member will benefit is not yet possible
   from the member's own score.
 - Adding a near-duplicate of an existing member HURTS. Model148 and Model141 agree
   almost everywhere, so together they carry two correlated votes into a min_votes 2
   rule and can outvote the correct minority. The ensemble wants different errors,
   not more of the same - the same lesson as Model147, measured from the other side.
